Selected work

Policy, plans and field projects across Nepal.

01

Bagmati Province Tourism Strategy

15-year strategy / 2021 to 2023

A roadmap for infrastructure, people, marketing, regulation and inclusive tourism. Covers roads and trails, training, marketing, rules and the role of local government.

02

Integrated Tourism Programme, Bhaktapur

Feasibility study / 2022

Tourism potential, a flagship destination and a framework for Bhaktapur Constituency No. 1. Maps tourism potential in the constituency and names one flagship destination to build first.

03

Tourism for Sustainable Development

UNDP and UN-WTO / 2002

National consultant and research specialist on a sustainable tourism master plan. Part of a national programme that ended in a long-term sustainable tourism plan.

04

Nepal Tourism Development Master Plan 2008

Deputy Team Leader / 2008

Product development, marketing, forecasting and Tourism Satellite Account orientation. Sets targets for new products, promotion and forecasting, with a clear owner for each.

05

Heritage Walks, Kathmandu

Conservation and tourism / 2010

Guided walks that raise conservation awareness and bring local income. Walks through the old core link temples, courtyards and craft streets, and keep visitors a night longer.

06

Second World Buddhist Summit 2004

Executive Director, LDT / 2004

Programme planning, finance, coordination of partners, monitoring and evaluation. A national event with many partners, run to a fixed budget and a monitored schedule.
